Support

We're happy to help with MyCareRoster.

MyCareRoster keeps your family's GPs, specialists, and care teams, and the details new-patient forms ask for, in one private place, built from your own Contacts. Their appointments stay in Calendar. If something isn't working or you have a question, get in touch and we'll get back to you.

Email MyCareRoster Support

support@mycareroster.com · We typically respond within a few business days. Please don't include health details or identifiers in your email.

Frequently asked questions

Who is the sample family?

June, Kate, and Isla are a fictional family that shows how MyCareRoster works. Choose Look Around a Sample Family on the welcome screen to explore them. They exist only in memory and are never saved or synced. Choose Turn Off Sample Data (also in Settings) to set up your own roster. In Settings, under Getting Started, Show Welcome Screen brings back the welcome screen, and Explore Sample Family brings back the sample.

Where do my providers come from?

From the Contacts app. Add Provider lists your Contacts list named "Medical" first, then your other contacts, and can create a new provider contact or start one from details you paste from a practice website, an email signature, or a business card. Each provider you add also goes into the Medical list, which MyCareRoster creates if you don't have one, so your providers stay together in Contacts. Edits you make in Contacts appear in MyCareRoster after you pull down on the roster to refresh.

Do I have to share all my contacts?

No. On iOS 18 and later you can share only some contacts with MyCareRoster, and it works fully with just your providers. To share another one, tap Share a Contact in Add Provider, type their name, and tap Share beside them. You can also choose several in the Settings app, under Apps > MyCareRoster > Contacts. Your family, teams, notes, and private profiles work without Contacts access at all.

How do I find a provider quickly?

Each family member's screen lists all their providers, each with an icon for their kind of care. On the roster, All Providers lists everyone in your care teams; search by name, specialty, or practice, or use Showing to see one family member's providers. Care teams are optional groups. See Finding your way around.

A contact couldn't be added to the Medical list. Why?

Contacts lists belong to one account, such as iCloud or Gmail. A contact stored in a different account from the Medical list can't join it, and MyCareRoster won't copy or duplicate it. It's still added as a provider; only the list step is skipped. To keep it in the list, move or recreate the contact in the same account as the Medical list.

A provider is marked "No longer in Contacts". What do I do?

Their contact was deleted, or, if you share only some contacts with MyCareRoster, it's no longer shared (iOS reports both the same way, so the label then says "Deleted, or no longer shared with MyCareRoster"). Taking a contact out of the Medical list doesn't do this. MyCareRoster keeps their notes and teams. In their team, tap the ••• button beside them and choose Reconnect to choose their contact, Share Again if you share only some contacts, or Remove from Team if you no longer need them there.

I can't find the Suffix or Job Title field in Contacts.

MyCareRoster shows a provider's credentials from the Suffix field and their job title from the Job Title field, but Contacts doesn't always show those fields. On iPhone or iPad, open the contact, tap Edit, scroll near the bottom, and tap Add Field. On a Mac, choose Contacts > Settings > Template and use Add Field. See Keeping details up to date.

How do appointments work?

Appointments stay in Apple's Calendar app, and MyCareRoster doesn't keep a copy. Each family member's screen lists their upcoming and past appointments with all their providers, and each provider's screen lists theirs. Add Appointment, on either screen, opens Calendar's event editor already filled in. If you don't know who an appointment is with yet, choose No Provider Yet and link it to a provider later. You can choose which calendar new appointments go in, in Settings under Appointments. See Appointments.

How do I paste a reminder from the practice?

Copy the whole reminder or confirmation from Messages, WhatsApp, or email. Then tap Paste in the Appointments section of the provider's screen, or, if you're not sure which provider it's from, Paste Reminder on the family member's Appointments card or in the roster's + menu. If you still don't know who it's with, choose Add Without a Provider. If it looks like it's for someone else, or looks like contact details instead, MyCareRoster asks what to do with it. MyCareRoster reads the date, time, and place, and offers to add, move, or delete the appointment. Nothing changes in Calendar until you confirm, and the message isn't kept. MyCareRoster can't send replies for you. See Pasting a reminder.

How do I share someone's details with a new clinic?

On the family member's screen, tap ••• and choose Share Summary. It makes a PDF of their providers, with their GP first, to send by Messages, Mail, or AirDrop, or to print. Turn on Include allergies, medicines, conditions, and procedures to add those from their private profile; MyCareRoster asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode first. Notes and health card, insurance, and hospital numbers are never included, and MyCareRoster keeps no copy. See Sharing a summary.

Can I see allergies and medicines without Face ID?

Yes, if you choose. In a family member's unlocked private profile, under Health, turn on Quick card without Face ID. Their allergies, medicines, conditions, and procedures then show on their screen without unlocking, on that device only, so anyone holding your unlocked phone can see them. Credentials and the rest of the profile stay locked. Tap Turn Off on the card to hide it again. See The quick card.

How do I take a provider off someone's screen?

Tap the ••• button beside the provider on that person's screen, or touch and hold them, and choose Remove from Kate's Roster (or Remove from Your Roster). It takes them out of that person's teams only. Their notes and other family members' teams are kept, their contact isn't deleted, and their appointments stay in Calendar. See Removing a provider from someone's roster.

Can I change the app's colours, or a provider's icon?

Yes. In Settings, under Appearance, tap Theme to choose Light, Dark, or Automatic, and Teal, Green, Violet, or Coral. To change a provider's icon, open the provider and, under Manage provider, tap Change on the Icon row. See Themes and A provider's icon.

How do I keep my iPhone and iPad in sync?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both, with iCloud and iCloud Keychain (Passwords and Keychain) turned on. Your roster syncs automatically, encrypted. A new device may need a moment for iCloud Keychain to deliver the encryption key; tap Sync saved care teams, or pull down on the roster, if your teams haven't appeared yet. If a provider's contact doesn't match exactly on both devices, MyCareRoster asks you to confirm the match.

Do private profiles sync?

Only if you turn on Settings > Private Profiles > Sync with iCloud on each device. Profiles are encrypted on your device before they are stored in iCloud. Turning sync off keeps them on the device and lets you delete the iCloud copy.

What does MyCareRoster Unlimited include?

The free version includes one family member, two care teams, and three providers in those teams, and it never expires. Private profiles, the quick card, Share Summary, notes, appointments, themes, and encrypted iCloud sync are included free. MyCareRoster Unlimited is a one-time purchase that removes the limits on family members, care teams, and providers. It isn't a subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same App Store account, then open Settings > Restore Purchases in MyCareRoster. Restore Purchases is also on the MyCareRoster Unlimited screen.

I have an offer code. How do I use it?

In MyCareRoster, open Settings and tap Redeem Code under Purchases (also on the MyCareRoster Unlimited screen), then enter the code in Apple's screen. MyCareRoster Unlimited turns on once Apple confirms it. Some codes can also be redeemed in the App Store app or through their link.

What is Support Info, and how do I send it?

If we ask for it: in MyCareRoster, open Settings and tap the version number seven times to show Diagnostics. Open Support Info, tap Copy All Support Info, and paste it into your email. It lists app, device, sync, and purchase details, but no names, contact details, notes, or health information, and nothing is sent unless you paste it. Tap the lock icon beside the version to hide Diagnostics again.

Enjoying MyCareRoster?

A review on the App Store helps other carers and families find it. Write a review, or choose Rate MyCareRoster in the app's Settings. If something isn't working, email us and we'll help.
