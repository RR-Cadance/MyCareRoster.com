1. Getting started

The sample family

The first time you open MyCareRoster, it shows a sample family: June, Kate, and Isla. They're fictional, and they show how everything works before you add anyone of your own. Explore as much as you like. Nothing about them is saved or synced, their details can't be changed, and their phone numbers, email addresses, and websites don't call, email, or open anything. Their appointments are samples too, and aren't in Calendar. Their private profiles still ask for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode, so you can see how that works too.

When you're ready, tap Turn Off Sample Data on the Sample Family card at the top of the roster. You can bring the sample back at any time from Settings, with Explore Sample Family. Your own roster isn't changed, and it returns when you turn the sample off again.

Already using MyCareRoster on another device? If your roster is waiting in iCloud, the card says Your roster is in iCloud. Tap Show My Roster.

Connect your Medical contacts

Your providers' names, phone numbers, and email addresses come from the Contacts app, from a contacts list named Medical. That way, there's one place to keep them current.

  1. Tap Allow Contacts Access, then allow access when iOS asks. MyCareRoster changes Contacts only when you ask it to create, add, or edit a provider.
  2. If you don't have a Medical list yet, tap Create Medical List. MyCareRoster creates an empty list named Medical in Contacts.
  3. Add your first family member (see Family members).

If you turned Contacts access off, MyCareRoster shows Contacts Access Is Off. Tap Open Settings and allow access for MyCareRoster. If you have more than one list named Medical, keep only one, then refresh MyCareRoster.

If you allowed access to only some contacts, MyCareRoster can't see the rest of your Medical list. It says Contacts access is limited on the roster. Tap Allow Full Access in Settings, choose Contacts, then Full Access.

2. Finding your way around

The roster, titled Your care network, is home. It lists your family members and, once providers are in their teams, All Providers: everyone in every care team, in one alphabetical list.

The buttons at the top of the roster, from left to right, are:

  • Paste Reminder, for an appointment reminder when you're not sure which provider it's from (see Pasting a reminder)
  • Add Family Member
  • Refresh, which reads your Medical contacts again
  • Settings

Each family member, care team, and team member has a ••• button with the things you can do to it. You can also touch and hold the row.

On iPhone, tap a family member to open their screen, and tap the back button to return. On iPad, and on the larger display of iPhone Duo, the roster stays beside whatever you open. The selected family member is highlighted, and you can hide the roster with the sidebar button.

3. Family members

Add everyone whose care you help with, including yourself.

  • Add: on the roster, tap Add Family Member and enter a name, such as Alex or Mum.
  • Rename or delete: tap the ••• button beside a family member, or touch and hold their name, then choose Rename or Delete. The same options are in the ••• menu on their own screen.

Deleting a family member removes their teams from MyCareRoster. Providers and their Contacts cards are not deleted.

A family member's screen shows, from the top:

  • their primary GP, with their credentials, their practice, and a button to call them (see Primary GP)
  • their private profile (see Private profiles)
  • their care teams, with the number of team members in each

The buttons at the top of their screen are Add Provider, New Team, and •••.

4. Adding providers

Providers are the contacts in your Medical list: GPs, specialists, clinics, therapists, pharmacies, or anyone else involved in care. Add them from a family member's screen or from one of their care teams.

  1. Open the family member and tap Add Provider, or open one of their teams and tap Add Team Member.
  2. Under Medical contacts, tap a provider.

From a team, the provider joins that team. From the family member's screen, they go into a team named Other, which MyCareRoster creates if needed. To put them in the right team later, use the ••• button beside them (see Care teams).

Someone who isn't in your Medical list yet

In the Add Provider screen, tap the + button, then choose:

  • Add Existing Contact to pick one or more people already in Contacts.
  • Create Provider Contact to make a new contact in Apple's contact editor.
  • Paste Provider Details to start a new contact from details you've copied, such as a practice webpage or an email signature (see Pasting provider details).

Either way, the contact joins your Medical list and this family member's roster, or the team you started from.

Contacts lists belong to one account, such as iCloud or Gmail. A contact stored in a different account from your Medical list can't join it, and MyCareRoster won't copy or duplicate it. Move or recreate the contact in the same account as the Medical list.

If you've given MyCareRoster limited access to Contacts, it can only add the contacts you've shared with it. To add others, allow full access in the Settings app.

5. Care teams

Care teams group the providers involved in one part of someone's care, such as Cardiology, Physio, or Rehabilitation. Each family member has their own teams, and the same provider can belong to as many teams as you like. The top of a team's screen shows whose team it is, such as Kate's care team.

  • Create a team: on the family member's screen, tap New Team and enter a name.
  • Add team members: open the team and tap Add Team Member (see Adding providers).
  • Move, copy, or remove a team member: tap the ••• button beside them, or touch and hold their name. Move to and Copy to list this family member's other teams, then other family members' teams. Remove from Team takes them out of this team only; their notes, role, specialty, and other teams are kept.
  • Rename or delete a team: tap the ••• button beside the team, or touch and hold it. On the team's own screen, use its ••• menu.

Deleting a team removes it from MyCareRoster. Providers and their Contacts cards are not deleted.

6. A provider's details

Tap a provider in a team, or in All Providers, to open their details. At the top are their name, with any credentials (such as MBBS FRACGP) on a smaller line below it, their job title from Contacts, and their practice. Under those, MyCareRoster shows who they're the primary GP for, or their specialty.

  • What the letters mean: tap to see what each credential after their name stands for. It also explains titles such as Professor, and why some surgeons are called Mr or Ms. Credentials are shown as written in Contacts; MyCareRoster doesn't check qualifications.
  • Get in touch: calls first, then messaging apps, emails, and websites, named with the labels in their contact card, such as Call Reception. A mobile number has Message beside its call button. If their card has a WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, or Messenger account, you can open a chat there.
  • Locations: every address in their contact card, and Open in Contacts to see or edit their full card. Changes you make there show in MyCareRoster when you close the card.
  • Appointments: their upcoming and past appointments in Calendar, with Add Appointment and Paste (see Appointments).
  • Notes: type in Add a note. It saves when you tap Done or leave the field, and Undo takes it back. Notes are private to MyCareRoster, and they're never written into Contacts. To change or delete a note, tap its ••• button, or touch and hold it.
  • Manage provider: set their Role to General Practitioner, Specialist, or Unclassified. For a specialist, type their specialty. It saves on its own, with Undo, and is shown under their name throughout the app.
  • Other contact details: fax and pager numbers, and anything else in their card that can't be called or opened. MyCareRoster never dials these. Tap the copy button beside one to paste it into a referral form.

7. Primary GP

Each family member can have one primary GP, shown at the top of their screen with a button to call them. The same GP can be primary for several family members.

  1. Make sure the GP is in at least one of the family member's care teams.
  2. Open the GP's details and, under Manage provider, set Role to General Practitioner.
  3. Turn on Primary GP for beside the family member's name. If someone else was their primary GP, the switch shows who it replaces.

To stop using a GP as primary, turn the same switch off. The call button uses the GP's first phone or mobile number (never a fax or pager) and its label from Contacts, for example Call Reception. If the number has no label, it says Call GP.

8. Appointments

Appointments stay in Apple's Calendar app. MyCareRoster shows each provider's appointments on their screen and helps you add and update them, but it doesn't keep a copy of them.

Adding an appointment

  1. On the provider's screen, tap Add Appointment.
  2. If this provider is in more than one family member's care teams, choose who it's for.
  3. The first time, MyCareRoster asks for access to your calendars, and, if you have more than one, which calendar new appointments go in. You can change this later in Settings, under Appointments.
  4. Choose where it is: one of the provider's addresses, Phone Consultation, Video Consultation, or Somewhere Else to type a place yourself.
  5. Calendar's event editor opens, filled in with the provider's name and specialty (or GP), the location, and, if you have more than one family member, who it's for. Set the date and time, change anything else you like, and tap Add.

MyCareRoster adds a link to the event, in its URL field or, if that's in use, its notes. The link is how MyCareRoster knows which provider the appointment is with.

Seeing appointments

A provider's screen lists their Upcoming and Past appointments from the last and next 12 months. Use Show Earlier Appointments or Show Later Appointments to look further. If MyCareRoster hasn't asked for Calendar access yet, tap Show Appointments. If Calendar access is off, tap Open Settings and allow it. MyCareRoster reads your calendars on this device; nothing is copied or uploaded.

Tap an appointment to see it, and change or delete it, without leaving MyCareRoster. Its ••• button has Show in Calendar, which opens it in the Calendar app, and Unlink if it isn't with this provider after all.

Appointments you added elsewhere

An event that mentions the provider's surname but wasn't added through MyCareRoster is listed as a Possible match. If it's theirs, tap its ••• button and choose Link to the provider. MyCareRoster asks who it's for and where it is, and if that changes the event's location, it asks you to confirm and adds a note of the earlier location to the event. After linking one, it offers to link other events like it.

If someone else sent you the event as an invitation, the other people invited may see the MyCareRoster link.

The sample family's appointments are samples to explore. They aren't in Calendar, and nothing is read from or written to Calendar while the sample family is shown.

9. Pasting a reminder

When a practice sends an appointment reminder, booking confirmation, change, or cancellation by text message or email, MyCareRoster can read it and update Calendar for you.

  1. In Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, or wherever it arrived, copy the whole message.
  2. On the provider's screen, tap Paste beside Reminder or confirmation text. If you're not sure which provider it's from, tap Paste Reminder at the top of the roster instead.
  3. Check what MyCareRoster found, then choose what to do.

MyCareRoster reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, and it doesn't keep the message.

Who it's from

When you paste from the roster, MyCareRoster suggests providers whose name or practice is in the message. It never matches by phone number or email address. You can also choose someone in Contacts who isn't in your Medical list yet (they're added to it), create a new contact from the message, or search all your providers.

Checking the details

The review shows what kind of message it is, such as a reminder, a cancellation notice, or an appointment that moved, with the date and time it found. It warns you when a date could be read two ways, or has already passed, and lets you set the date if the message doesn't give one. If the provider is in more than one family member's care teams, choose who the appointment is for.

Choose the location from the provider's addresses in Contacts, an address in the message, or Phone Consultation or Video Consultation. For a video consultation, you can add the message's video link to the event's notes after checking its address.

Updating Calendar

  • A new appointment: Add to Calendar opens Calendar's event editor, filled in.
  • A moved appointment: if there's already an appointment with the provider nearby, MyCareRoster suggests moving it instead, so Calendar doesn't keep both. Update Appointment shows the time and location now and after, for you to confirm.
  • A cancellation: Delete from Calendar finds the appointment and asks before deleting it.

Nothing changes in Calendar until you confirm.

Replying, calling, and links

MyCareRoster can't send replies. If the message says, for example, to reply Y to confirm, it shows what to reply, and you reply in the app the message came in. If it asks you to call, MyCareRoster offers to call the number, and adds a note of the call to the appointment.

Scam messages often copy appointment reminders. MyCareRoster shows a link's full address and asks before opening it or adding it to an event. Only open links you expect from that practice.

If the message gives the provider's name more fully than Contacts does, such as a middle name or a title, MyCareRoster offers Update the Name in Contacts. Only the title and names change, and a middle name the message leaves out is kept.

10. Pasting provider details

Start a new provider contact from details you've copied, instead of typing them in.

  1. Copy the provider's details from a practice webpage, an email signature, or a contact card. For a business card, open it in Camera or Photos, touch and hold the card's text, then tap Copy.
  2. Open a family member or team, tap Add Provider or Add Team Member, tap +, then choose Paste Provider Details.
  3. Tap Paste. If the text names several people, choose the one to add.
  4. Check the details. You can change the title, names, credentials, specialty, department, role, and practice, and choose which phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, and websites to include. Practices often share phone numbers and emails between providers, so include only the ones you want on this contact.
  5. Tap Continue in Contacts. Apple's contact editor opens, filled in, so you can check everything before it's saved. Tap Done.

The new contact is added to your Medical list and to the family member's roster, or to the team. MyCareRoster doesn't keep the pasted text.

When the provider is already in Contacts

If a contact with the same name exists, MyCareRoster offers it under Already in Contacts?, so you don't create a duplicate. Tap Use to add it as it is, or Update with Pasted Details to choose what to change. Adding something new starts turned on; replacing something already on the contact starts off. Nothing is removed, and nothing is saved until you confirm.

Credentials and specialty

Credentials, such as MBBS FRACGP, are saved in the contact's Suffix field. MyCareRoster keeps the specialty for the provider, and in Contacts it's saved as the contact's job title unless you enter a role.

11. Keeping details up to date

Names, credentials, job titles, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, organizations, and addresses always come from Contacts. Update a provider's card in Contacts, then tap Refresh on the roster to see the change. MyCareRoster also reads your Medical list whenever it starts up. Your roles, specialties, notes, teams, and primary GP choices stay as they are.

Fields Contacts may hide

MyCareRoster reads a provider's title (Prefix), middle name, credentials (Suffix), job title, and department. Contacts doesn't always show the Suffix and Job Title fields, but you can add them. Credentials in the Suffix field are what What the letters mean explains, and they keep providers sorted by surname.

  • iPhone and iPad: open the contact, tap Edit, scroll near the bottom, and tap Add Field. The list offers only the fields the card isn't already showing.
  • Mac: in Contacts, choose Contacts > Settings > Template, then use Add Field to choose a field, such as Suffix or Job Title. The template sets which fields every card shows.

When a provider leaves your Medical list

If a provider is removed from your Medical list, or their contact is deleted, MyCareRoster keeps their notes and settings but hides their contact details and removes them from All Providers. In their teams, they're marked Not in your Medical list. Tap the ••• button beside them:

  • Repair offers Add Back to Medical if their contact is still in Contacts, or Choose a Different Contact. Their notes, role, specialty, and teams are kept.
  • Remove from Team takes them out of that team. Their contact isn't deleted.

12. Private profiles

Each family member has a private profile for the details forms and clinics ask for. Open it from the family member's screen, then tap Authenticate to View Profile and use Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

What a profile holds

  • Identity: names, including their legal name and the name they like to be called. The preferred name is their name throughout the app.
  • Credentials: government identifiers, insurance coverage, and care organization identifiers, such as a hospital record number (MRN).
  • Health: allergies, medicines, conditions, and procedures, each with a name and details.

Every field is optional except the preferred name, which is the family member's name in your roster. Add what you know now and the rest later.

Adding and changing details

Tap a section, such as Medicines. If it's empty, it opens ready to fill in. Otherwise, tap Edit, make your changes, and tap Save.

Copying into a form

Open a section and tap the copy button beside a value, then paste it into a form. Copied values stay on this device (they don't go to your other devices through Universal Clipboard) and clear from the clipboard after two minutes.

Hidden numbers

Credential numbers are hidden until you turn on Show credential values. If the profile has been open for more than five minutes, it asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode again first.

Staying locked

An unlocked profile stays open for five minutes. After that, revealed numbers hide again, and MyCareRoster asks you to authenticate before you open, change, or copy anything more. Anything you're typing in an editor is kept. Numbers also hide as soon as you leave the app, and an open section is covered in the app switcher. It's locked again each time you open it.

Private profiles stay on this device unless you turn on iCloud sync (see iCloud and your other devices).

13. iCloud and your other devices

Your roster of family members, care teams, roles, specialties, notes, and primary GPs syncs automatically between your iPhone and iPad through your private iCloud account. It's encrypted on your device first, with a key kept in iCloud Keychain, so iCloud holds only encrypted data. Only providers you've set up are included: those in a team, chosen as a primary GP, given a role, specialty, or note, or reconnected to a contact.

To sync, sign in to the same Apple Account on each device, with iCloud and iCloud Keychain (Passwords and Keychain) turned on. Contact details aren't copied between devices: each device reads them from its own Contacts, so your Medical list needs to be on each device too, for example through iCloud Contacts. So that your devices can match providers, the encrypted roster includes their names and organizations, and a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of their phone numbers and email addresses. See the Privacy Policy.

Setting up a new device

A new device may need a moment for iCloud Keychain to deliver the encryption key. If your teams haven't appeared, tap Sync saved care teams or Refresh. If a private profile says Waiting for iCloud Keychain, check that iCloud Keychain is on, keep the device online and unlocked for a few minutes, and tap Try Again.

Providers to reconnect

If MyCareRoster can't be sure which contact on this device matches a provider saved on another device, it lists them under Providers to reconnect. Tap one and choose the same person. Similar names in your Medical list are shown first; contacts outside it are listed under In Contacts, Not in Medical, or tap Choose in Contacts…. Confirm with Connect Same Provider, or Add to Medical and Connect for a contact outside your Medical list. Their notes, role, and teams are restored. Nothing is matched until you confirm.

If they're no longer needed, use their ••• button and choose Remove from All Teams. Their contact isn't deleted.

Syncing private profiles

Private profiles don't sync unless you choose to. To open them on your other devices, turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, under Private Profiles, on each device. They're encrypted on the device before they're stored in iCloud.

When you turn sync off, choose Stop Syncing Only to keep the iCloud copy, or Stop Syncing and Delete from iCloud to remove it. Either way, the profiles stay on this device. Turn sync off on your other devices first, or they'll upload their copy again.

14. Free version and MyCareRoster Unlimited

The free version never expires. It includes:

  • one family member
  • two care teams
  • three providers across those teams (a provider in more than one team counts once)

Private profiles, notes, appointments, pasting, and encrypted iCloud sync are included free.

MyCareRoster Unlimited removes the limits on family members, care teams, and providers. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and works on all your devices signed in to the same App Store account. When you reach a limit, MyCareRoster tells you which one and offers See MyCareRoster Unlimited. You can also find it in Settings, under Purchases.

If adding a provider from a family member's screen would need a new Other team and there's no room for one, MyCareRoster asks which of their teams to add them to instead.

To restore your purchase on a new device, sign in with the same App Store account and tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the MyCareRoster Unlimited screen.

15. Settings

Tap Settings on the roster.

  • Sample Family: Explore Sample Family or Turn Off Sample Data.
  • Private Profiles: Sync with iCloud.
  • Contacts: whether MyCareRoster has full, limited, or no access to Contacts, with a button to change it in the Settings app.
  • Appointments: Calendar for New Appointments, the calendar Add Appointment uses. Choose Calendar's Default or any calendar you can add events to. This choice is kept on this device only, and you can still pick another calendar in the event editor.
  • Purchases: your MyCareRoster Unlimited status, See MyCareRoster Unlimited, and Restore Purchases.
  • About: the version, Privacy Policy, Support, Rate MyCareRoster, and Send Feedback, which opens an email to us. Please don't include health details or identifiers.

16. Deleting your information

Deleting MyCareRoster from a device removes what it stored on that device. Copies in iCloud remain until you remove them.

MyCareRoster's Diagnostics section has tools for this. To show it, open Settings and tap the version number seven times. To hide it again, tap the lock icon beside the version.

  • Delete from iCloud removes the encrypted iCloud copy of your roster, your private profiles, or both. This device keeps its own copy and stops syncing it until you tap Resume iCloud Uploads or hide Diagnostics.
  • Delete Everything permanently deletes every family member, team, provider setting, note, and private profile from iCloud and this device, along with the encryption keys. Delete MyCareRoster from your other devices first, or they'll upload their copy again.

Both ask for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Neither changes your contacts, which stay in Contacts, or your appointments, which stay in Calendar.

Diagnostics also has Support Info, which we may ask you to copy into an email if you need help. See Support.
