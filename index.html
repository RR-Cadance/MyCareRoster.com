Everything they'll ask for. In one place.

When you're filling in a new-patient form, answering a doctor's questions, or stepping in for someone you love, the details you need are scattered across Contacts, emails, insurance apps, cards, and memory. MyCareRoster brings them together in one calm, private place, so they're ready when the pressure's on.

For the new-patient form

Fill in forms without the scramble

At home or in the waiting room, each family member's legal names, health card and insurance numbers, hospital record numbers, allergies, medicines, conditions, and procedures are ready to copy, one field at a time.

Copied values stay on the device you're using and clear from the clipboard after two minutes. A provider's fax number is there to copy too, for referral forms.

For the appointment

Every specialist, answered

When a doctor asks who else is treating you, or who should receive a report, every specialist on each care team is right there, with their practice and contact details.

"What the letters mean" explains the credentials after a provider's name, such as MBBS, FRACP, or FACS.

For caring for family

Everyone you care for

Keep a roster for each person you look after: a parent, a partner, a child, or yourself. Each family member's primary GP sits at the top of their screen, with reception one tap away.

Appointments stay in Calendar

Paste the reminder. It's in Calendar.

Add an appointment from any provider's screen. Calendar's editor opens with their name filled in, and the address, phone consultation, or video consultation you choose. Each provider's upcoming and past appointments are listed on their screen.

Paste a reminder or confirmation from a text message or email. MyCareRoster reads the date, time, and place, and offers to add the appointment, move it, or delete a cancelled one. Nothing changes until you confirm, and MyCareRoster doesn't keep a copy of your appointments.

Less to remember

The details, plus your notes

Call or email any provider with one tap, text a mobile, or open their website. If their contact card has a WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, or Messenger account, start a chat there.

Keep private notes beside each provider, such as parking instructions or the best time to call. Notes save automatically.

Private by design

Locked with Face ID

  • No sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Private profiles open with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Credentials stay hidden until you reveal them.
  • Your roster syncs between your devices through your private iCloud account, encrypted on your device first.
  • Private profiles stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync, and are encrypted before they leave it.

Read the privacy policy

Built for the way care really works

  • Care teams

    Organize providers into care teams for each family member: cardiology, physio, the local hospital, or anything else. Move or copy a provider to another team in a few taps.

  • Built from your Contacts

    Your providers come from a Contacts list named "Medical". MyCareRoster can create the list, create a new provider contact, add contacts you already have, or start a contact from details you paste from a practice website, an email signature, or a business card. Nothing is changed in Contacts unless you ask.

  • Always up to date

    Update a phone number in Contacts and MyCareRoster shows it after a refresh.

  • Easy to read

    Large, high-contrast type, with support for Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.
